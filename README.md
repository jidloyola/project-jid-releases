## What is Project J.I.D.?

Project J.I.D. is a Windows desktop app built for DepEd teachers who want a clearer, faster picture of how their students are doing — and what to do about it.

It brings together attendance, grades, risk profiling, and intervention tracking in one place, so you spend less time digging through spreadsheets and more time actually helping students.

Whether you're a **class adviser** managing a full homeroom, or a **subject teacher** monitoring performance in your specific class, Project J.I.D. adapts to how you work. You decide how much of it you use — it can be as simple as a grade tracker or as powerful as a full intervention management system.

---

## Who is it for?

**Class Advisers** get the full picture: attendance trends, grade monitoring across all subjects, student risk levels, and a live Cockpit that surfaces who needs attention first.

**Subject Teachers** can use the app in a focused mode — track grades and performance for their subject, flag at-risk students, and run targeted intervention campaigns without needing to manage the full homeroom dataset.

The app scales to what you need. Start small, expand when you're ready.

## Installation

1. Download the installer (`.exe`) from the [Releases page](https://github.com/jidloyola/project-jid-releases/releases)
2. Run the installer — it will install the app and create a desktop shortcut
3. Launch **Project J.I.D.** and activate with your license key
4. Go to **Settings** and enter your name, school, section, and school year

---

## First-Time Setup

When you open the app for the first time, go to **Settings** (gear icon) and fill in:

- **School Name** — your school's official name
- **Section** — your class section (e.g. Grade 12 DEIMOS)
- **School Year** — e.g. 2026–2027
- **Class Adviser Name** — used on certificates and reports
- **School Head** — for report headers
- **Subjects Handled** — list your subjects, comma-separated

These fields appear on all generated documents and certificates. You only need to set them once.

## Key Features

**Adviser Cockpit with a "Today" Queue** — A priority-ranked dashboard that surfaces your most at-risk students automatically, opening with one ordered worklist: newly at-risk students, overdue follow-ups, repeat incidents, and unacknowledged parent notices — each actionable right from the list.

**Find Anything with Ctrl+K** — Press Ctrl+K anywhere and start typing: a student's name opens their case file, a view name jumps there, and common actions (log an intervention, import data, back up) run directly.

**Grade Analytics & Strengthened-SHS Support** — Reads the Strengthened SHS (MATATAG) class records and the three-term adviser workbook, showing the file's own Term and Final Grades. Grade-band distributions, component performance, Term-Exam MPS, and top/failing lists per section — with Crosstrack and PureTech Pro programs handled correctly.

**Written Reprimand & Parent Notices** — Formal, fairly-worded discipline documents one click from any logged incident: a student-addressed Written Reprimand with offense history and due-process wording, plus a five-rung parent notice ladder — all with acknowledgment tracking and follow-up reminders.

**Drop Files Anywhere** — Drag an SF2 or class record onto any screen to import it, including month-tabbed SF2 workbooks (pick which months to load) and macro-enabled .xlsm files.

**Student Risk Profiling** — Risk levels reflect both attendance patterns and academic performance. Every badge shows a reason label *(Academic)*, *(Attendance)*, or *(Both)* so you always know what to act on.

**SF2 Attendance Parsing** — Upload the Rick Creative SF2-SHS Excel template. The app reads all attendance symbols (⬒ Late, ⬓ Cutting, HD Half Day, X Absent) and computes correct rates and absent-day counts.

**Intervention Campaigns** — Create, run, and track targeted intervention campaigns for at-risk student groups. Purpose-built for action research — documents what was done, when, and whether it worked.

**MPS Suite & Item Analysis** — Upload class records to generate a full Mean Percentage Score breakdown. Item Analysis identifies which test questions students consistently got wrong.

**Parent Communication Log** — Log parent contacts (phone, SMS, email, in-person, home visit) directly from the student card. Contacts appear in the unified intervention history.

**Reports & Certificates** — Generate grade slips, certificates, parent letters, and conference packs pre-filled with your school identity. Supports both quarterly (Q1–Q4) and trimester (T1–T2–T3) grading structures.

**Knowledge Base** — Nine built-in reference articles explain how every system works: risk calculation (with a live simulator), Option B EMA policy, attendance formulas, Honor Roll criteria, and more.

**School Year Timeline** — Live sidebar showing the SY 2026–2027 three-term calendar with today's position, milestone countdowns, and SF2 upload status per month.

## Auto-Updates

The app updates itself. On every launch it checks for a newer version. If found, a dialog will offer to download it in the background. Once downloaded, choose **Restart Now** or **Install on Next Close**.

To check manually: **Help → Check for Updates…**
