# Project J.I.D.
### Just-in-Time Intervention Dashboard — for DepEd Senior High School teachers

**Latest version:** v4.0.0 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Platform:** Windows 10/11 (Desktop) &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Cost:** Free for DepEd use

Project J.I.D. helps class advisers and subject teachers turn their everyday DepEd files — SF2 attendance, class records, and consolidated grades — into a clear picture of who needs help, and why. It flags at-risk learners early, tracks grades and attendance, and generates ready-to-print reports, parent slips, and intervention logs.

---

## ⬇️ Download

**[Download the latest version →](https://github.com/jidloyola/project-jid-releases/releases/latest)**

1. Open the link above and download the `.exe` installer under **Assets**.
2. Double-click the downloaded file to install.
3. If Windows shows a **“Windows protected your PC”** screen, click **More info → Run anyway** (this appears because the app is distributed directly, not through the Microsoft Store).
4. Launch **Project J.I.D.** from your Start menu or desktop.

> 🔄 **Automatic updates:** Once installed, the app checks for new versions on launch and offers to update itself — you won’t need to come back here for future releases.

## ✨ What’s new in v4.0.0

**Grade Analytics, Strengthened SHS support, and a clearer honor system.**

- **Grade Analytics built into the dashboard.** See your class at a glance — average, highest and lowest, how many are passing, the spread across performance bands, and a breakdown of Written Works, Performance Tasks, and Examinations (with the Term-Exam Mean Percentage Score). Subject teachers get this per subject; advisers also get a homeroom view based on the General Average.
- **Reads the new Strengthened SHS (MATATAG) class records.** Workbooks that keep Term 1, 2, and 3 on separate sheets now load correctly — only the terms with actual scores are read — and the ST1 / ST2 / Term-Exam columns flow through to the MPS Suite.
- **One tap to the learner.** Click a student’s name anywhere in the analytics to jump straight to their profile; click the “Needs support” card to open the list of learners below passing; click MPS to open the MPS Suite.
- **Back & Forward buttons.** Move through the app like a browser — on-screen ← / → buttons, the Alt+← / Alt+→ keys, or your mouse’s side buttons.
- **For subject teachers.** You can now open Analytics and Sections too. The old “Attendance” tab is now **“Attendance & Risk”** to reflect that risk levels combine attendance, behavior, and grades.
- **Honor recognition updated.** The app now uses the single **Academic Excellence Award** — a general average of **at least 89.5 with no grade below 80** — across the Honor Roll, reports, and student profiles.

### Also fixed
- Selecting a section in the Subject-Teacher Grades view is now reliable.
- Section reports (Academic, Attendance, Intervention) open correctly when only that section’s data is loaded.

## 💻 Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
- About 300 MB of free disk space
- No internet required to use it day-to-day (only for downloading and updates)

## 🔒 Your data stays with you

Project J.I.D. runs entirely on your computer. Your students’ attendance and grades are stored locally on your device — nothing is uploaded to any server.
