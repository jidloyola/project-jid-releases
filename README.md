## What is Project J.I.D.?

Project J.I.D. is a Windows desktop app built for DepEd teachers who want a clearer, faster picture of how their students are doing — and what to do about it.

It brings together attendance from SF2, grades from class records and consolidated sheets, student risk profiling, and intervention tracking in one place — so you spend less time digging through spreadsheets and more time actually helping students.

---

## Key Features

**Adviser Cockpit** — A priority-ranked dashboard that surfaces your most at-risk students automatically. Combines attendance, grade, and behavioral signals into a single view.

**Student Risk Profiling** — Risk levels reflect both attendance patterns and academic performance. Every badge shows a reason label *(Academic)*, *(Attendance)*, or *(Both)* so you always know what to act on.

**SF2 Attendance Parsing** — Upload the Rick Creative SF2-SHS Excel template. The app reads all attendance symbols (⬒ Late, ⬓ Cutting, HD Half Day, X Absent) and computes correct rates and absent-day counts.

**Intervention Campaigns** — Create, run, and track targeted intervention campaigns for at-risk student groups. Purpose-built for action research — documents what was done, when, and whether it worked.

**MPS Suite & Item Analysis** — Upload class records to generate a full Mean Percentage Score breakdown. Item Analysis identifies which test questions students consistently got wrong.

**Parent Communication Log** — Log parent contacts (phone, SMS, email, in-person, home visit) directly from the student card. Contacts appear in the unified intervention history.

**Reports & Certificates** — Generate grade slips, certificates, parent letters, and conference packs pre-filled with your school identity. Supports both quarterly (Q1–Q4) and trimester (T1–T2–T3) grading structures.

**Knowledge Base** — Nine built-in reference articles explain how every system works: risk calculation (with a live simulator), Option B EMA policy, attendance formulas, Honor Roll criteria, and more.

**School Year Timeline** — Live sidebar showing the SY 2026–2027 three-term calendar with today's position, milestone countdowns, and SF2 upload status per month.

## Installation

1. Download the installer (`.exe`) from the [Releases page](https://github.com/jidloyola/project-jid-releases/releases)
2. Run the installer — it will install the app and create a desktop shortcut
3. Launch **Project J.I.D.** and activate with your license key
4. Go to **Settings** and enter your name, school, section, and school year

---

## Auto-Updates

The app updates itself. On every launch it checks for a newer version. If found, a dialog will offer to download it in the background. Once downloaded, choose **Restart Now** or **Install on Next Close**.

To check manually: **Help → Check for Updates…**
